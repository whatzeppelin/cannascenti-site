Step 1 of 5 — The Basics

Everything you need
in 90 seconds.

Before you walk into a dispensary, you only need to know three things. Here they are.

🔬
THC & CBD
THC is the compound responsible for psychoactive effects. CBD is non-psychoactive — it reduces anxiety and inflammation without impairment. Most products contain both. The ratio matters as much as the percentage.
🌿
Indica · Sativa · Hybrid
Indica = relaxing, body-focused. Sativa = energizing, cerebral. Hybrid = in between. But these labels are a starting point, not a guarantee. Terpene profile is a more reliable predictor of your actual experience.
✦
Terpenes
The aromatic compounds that give each strain its smell — and largely determine its effect. Myrcene makes you sleepy. Limonene lifts your mood. Pinene keeps you clear-headed. We’ll map the ones relevant to you in Step 3.
