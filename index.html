Why I built this

I've been selling cannabis in Los Angeles for over 12 years. I know the floor — the moments when a customer is standing there, wallet out, and genuinely doesn't know what they want. I was tired of not having a tool good enough to help them properly.

I built Cannascenti for that exact moment. The product scanner, the strain database, the terpene profiles — all of it came out of real conversations I've had at Erba Sawtelle. When someone asks me what the difference is between myrcene and limonene, I needed something I could actually show them — not just explain.

Everything here is documented, sourced, and built on real cannabis knowledge. Not crowdsourced vibes. Not affiliate marketing. Just the deepest reference I could build on a plant I've spent my career understanding, with the credibility of 12 years working it every day in the LA market.

— Michael Encinas, Founder
