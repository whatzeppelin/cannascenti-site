faq

questions, answered.

Is there a contract or commitment? ▾
No contracts. Month-to-month plans can be cancelled anytime. Annual plans are billed upfront and non-refundable after 14 days, but you can cancel renewal at any time.
How long does setup take? ▾
Budtender Pro is ready the moment you sign up. The white-label quiz (Growth and above) typically takes 3–5 business days once you send us your product catalog.
What do you need from us to set up the white-label quiz? ▾
Just your product catalog (strains and SKUs), your logo, and any brand colors. We handle the mapping, configuration, and testing — and deliver a QR code and embed link you can use immediately.
Can I try it before paying? ▾
Yes — book a 20-minute demo and we'll walk you through everything live. You can also try the public quiz and Budtender Pro on this site right now. No credit card needed until you're ready to go live.
What if I have multiple locations? ▾
Starter and Growth cover a single location. For multi-location groups or MSOs, the Enterprise plan is built for you — get in touch and we'll put together a custom quote.
