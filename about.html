✦ The Founder

Michael Encinas

I've been selling cannabis in Los Angeles for over a decade. In that time I've worked with thousands of people — patients, recreational users, first-timers, veterans, people from all over the world — all looking for the same thing: the right product for what they're actually going through.

Most of them didn't know how to ask for it. Most budtenders didn't have the tools to help them figure it out. That gap is what Cannascenti is built to close.

I'm currently a budtender at Erba Sawtelle in Los Angeles. I built this because I needed it on the floor — and because I'm convinced that better education leads to better experiences, better customers, and better business for dispensaries.

✦ Why Cannascenti

The problem is always the same.

A customer walks in. They don't know what they want, or they think they do and they're wrong. They point at something on the menu and guess. The budtender is busy. Nobody wins.

Cannascenti gives dispensaries a tool that does the education automatically — a personalized strain finder, an AI cannabis guide, a 394-strain encyclopedia, and a budtender training platform. Everything a floor staff member needs to give every customer a real recommendation instead of a guess.

The cannabis industry deserves better tools. So do the people working in it.

✦ The Platform

What we've built so far.

Mary Jane — AI cannabis guide trained on deep strain, terpene, and cannabinoid knowledge. She answers questions the way a great budtender would: directly, with an opinion.

Strain Finder — a 6-question quiz that matches customers to strains based on how they want to feel, not what's trending. 394 strains, 6 profiles, real recommendations.

Product Scanner — point your camera at any cannabis product and get a full intelligence briefing: terpenes, potency, dates, COA reference, and a strain comparison.

Budtender Pro — training quizzes and reference tools built for dispensary staff. Because knowledge on the floor directly affects the sale.

Encyclopedia — deep-dive reference covering strains, terpenes, cannabinoids, concentrates, edibles, and consumption methods.
