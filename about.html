about

I didn't build this from a boardroom.
I built it from behind the counter.

Three years on the floor at one of LA's top dispensaries. Thousands of customers. The same question, over and over: "What should I get?" Cannascenti is the tool that should have always existed.

the founder

Michael Encinas

I was there on day one of a dispensary grand opening in Los Angeles. I've spent years on the floor since — talking to real customers every single day. First-timers, veterans, patients, people from all over the world, all walking in with the same look: they don't know what they want, and they're hoping someone behind the counter does.

Most of the time, that someone was me. And I learned fast that a great recommendation isn't just about knowing cannabis — it's about knowing how to translate what a person actually needs into the right product. That takes time. That takes tools. Most dispensaries have neither.

I built Cannascenti because I lived this problem. Not from the outside — from behind the counter at Erba Sawtelle, one of LA's top dispensaries. Every feature on this platform exists because I needed it on the floor and it didn't exist anywhere else.

I'm not a tech founder who studied your industry from the outside. I know what it's like to have a line at the counter, a customer who can't decide, and a staff member who doesn't have the knowledge to close the sale. I know what actually works on the floor — because I'm still on it.

what we've built so far.

Mary Jane
AI cannabis guide trained on deep strain, terpene, and cannabinoid knowledge. She answers questions the way a great budtender would: directly, with an opinion.
Strain Finder
